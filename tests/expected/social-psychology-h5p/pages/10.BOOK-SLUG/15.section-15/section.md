glossary:
  - term: "Accommodation"
    definition: "When existing schemas change on the basis of new information"
  - term: "Actor-observer bias or difference"
    definition: "When we tend to make more personal attributions for the behavior of others than we do for ourselves and to make more situational attributions for our own behavior than for the behavior of others"
  - term: "Additive task"
    definition: "A task where the inputs of each group member are added together to create the group performance, and the expected performance of the group is the sum of group members’ individual inputs"
  - term: "Adjourning stage"
    definition: "When group members prepare for the group to end"
  - term: "Affect heuristic"
    definition: "The tendency to rely on automatically occurring affective responses to stimuli to guide our judgments of them"
  - term: "Affect"
    definition: "The feelings we experience as part of our everyday lives"
  - term: "Affective forecasting"
    definition: "Our attempts to predict how future events will make us feel"
  - term: "Aggression"
    definition: "Behavior that is intended to harm another individual who does not wish to be harmed"
  - term: "Agreeableness"
    definition: "A tendency to be good natured, cooperative, and trusting"
  - term: "Altruism"
    definition: "Any behavior that is designed to increase another person’s welfare, and particularly those actions that do not seem to provide a direct reward to the person who performs them"
  - term: "Altruistic or prosocial personality"
    definition: "An individual difference variable that relates to the likelihood of helping others across many different situations"
  - term: "Anchoring and adjustment"
    definition: "The tendency to weight initial information too heavily and thereby insufficiently move our judgment away from it"
  - term: "Anxious/ambivalent attachment style"
    definition: "When children become overly dependent on the parents and continually seek more affection from them than they can give"
  - term: "Arbitration"
    definition: "A type of third-party intervention that avoids negotiation as well as the necessity of any meetings between the parties in conflict"
  - term: "Assimilation"
    definition: "A process in which our existing knowledge influences new conflicting information to better fit with our existing knowledge, thus reducing the likelihood of schema change"
  - term: "Associational learning"
    definition: "When an object or event comes to be associated with a natural response, such as an automatic behavior or a positive or negative emotion"
  - term: "Attachment style"
    definition: "Individual differences in how people relate to others in close relationships"
  - term: "A"
    definition: "**ttitude** is a knowledge representation that includes primarily our liking or disliking of a person, thing, or group"
  - term: "Attitude strength"
    definition: "The importance of an attitude, as assessed by how quickly it comes to mind"
  - term: "Attribution"
    definition: "The process of assigning causes to behaviors"
  - term: "Attributional style"
    definition: "The type of attributions that we tend to make for events that occur to us."
  - term: "Authoritarianism"
    definition: "An individual difference variable characterized by a tendency to prefer things to be simple rather than complex and to hold traditional values"
  - term: "Automatic cognition"
    definition: "Thinking that occurs out of our awareness, quickly, and without taking much effort"
  - term: "Autonomy-oriented help"
    definition: "The belief that, given the appropriate tools, recipients can help themselves"
  - term: "Availability heuristic"
    definition: "The tendency to make judgments of the frequency of an event, or the likelihood that an event will occur, on the basis of the ease with which the event can be retrieved from memory"
  - term: "Avoidant attachment style"
    definition: "When children are unable to relate to the parents at all, becoming distant, fearful, and cold"
  - term: "Bait-and-switch technique"
    definition: "A persuasion attempt in which the target is offered one product at a very low price and yet the product at the low price is not actually available"
  - term: "Base rates"
    definition: "The likelihood that events occur across a large population"
  - term: "Basking in the reflected glory"
    definition: "When we use and advertise our ingroups’ positive achievements to boost our self-esteem"
  - term: "Behavioral measures"
    definition: "Measures designed to directly assess what people do"
  - term: "Bias blind spot"
    definition: "The tendency to believe that our own judgments are less susceptible to the influence of bias than those of others"
  - term: "Black sheep effect"
    definition: "The strong devaluation of ingroup members who threaten the positive image and identity of the ingroup"
  - term: "Blaming the victim"
    definition: "Interpreting the negative outcomes that occur to others internally so that it seems that they deserved them"
  - term: "Bogus pipeline procedure"
    definition: "A procedure, designed to elicit more honest responses, in which an experimenter first convinces participants that they have access to their “true” beliefs"
  - term: "Catharsis"
    definition: "The idea that engaging in less harmful aggressive actions will reduce the tendency to aggress later in a more harmful way"
  - term: "Causal attribution"
    definition: "The process of trying to determine the causes of people’s behavior"
  - term: "Central traits"
    definition: "Characteristics that have a very strong influence on our impressions of others"
  - term: "Charismatic leaders"
    definition: "Leaders who are enthusiastic, committed, and self-confident; who tend to talk about the importance of group goals at a broad level; and who make personal sacrifices for the group"
  - term: "Coercive power"
    definition: "The ability to dispense punishments"
  - term: "Cognitive accessibility"
    definition: "The extent to which a schema is activated in memory and thus likely to be used in information processing"
  - term: "Cognitive dissonance"
    definition: "The discomfort that occurs when we respond in ways that we see as inconsistent"
  - term: "Cognitive heuristics"
    definition: "Information-processing rules of thumb that enable us to think in ways that are quick and easy but that may sometimes lead to error"
  - term: "Cognitive reappraisal"
    definition: "Altering an emotional state by reinterpreting the meaning of the triggering situation or stimulus"

  - term: "Collectivism"
    definition: "Cultural norms that indicate that people should be more fundamentally connected with others and thus are more oriented toward interdependence"
  - term: "Commitment"
    definition: "The feelings and actions that keep partners working together to maintain the relationship"
  - term: "Common ingroup identity"
    definition: "The experience of social identity that occurs when differences in social grouping at one level are reduced by perceived similarities on a second, superordinate category"
  - term: "Communal relationships"
    definition: "Close relationships in which partners suspend their need for equity and exchange, giving support to the partner in order to meet his or her needs, and without consideration of the costs to themselves"
  - term: "Companionate love"
    definition: "Love that is based on friendship, mutual attraction, common interests, mutual respect, and concern for each other’s welfare"
  - term: "Compensatory (or averaging) task"
    definition: "A task where the group input is combined such that the performance of the individuals is averaged rather than added"
  - term: "Competition"
    definition: "The attempt to gain as many of the limited rewards as possible for ourselves, while reducing the likelihood of success for the other parties"
  - term: "Conceptual variables"
    definition: "The characteristics that we are trying to measure"
  - term: "Conditioning"
    definition: "The ability to connect stimuli (things or events in the environment) with responses (behaviors or other actions)"
  - term: "Confirmation bias"
    definition: "The tendency for people to seek out and favor information that confirms their expectations and beliefs"
  - term: "Conflict"

  - term: "Conformity"
    definition: "The change in beliefs, opinions, and behaviors as a result of our perceptions about what other people believe or do"

  - term: "Conscientiousness"

  - term: "Contact hypothesis"

  - term: "Contingency model of leadership effectiveness"
    definition: "A model of leadership effectiveness that focuses on both person variables and situational variables"

  - term: "Controlled cognition"

  - term: "Correlational research"
    definition: "Research designed to search for and test hypotheses about the relationships between two or more variables"
  - term: "Correspondence bias"

  - term: "Counterfactual thinking"

  - term: "Cover story"

  - term: "Criterion task"

  - term: "Culture of honor"

  - term: "Cyberbullying"
